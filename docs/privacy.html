Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Atlas is local-first. Your financial data stays on your device unless you choose optional iCloud sync. Atlas does not operate servers for core features and does not sell your information.

Information We Collect

Atlas does not require an account. We do not collect, receive, or have access to the financial information you enter — account balances, transactions, bills, debts, goals, or your name — unless you contact us by email.

Where Your Data Lives

  • On your device (default). All data you enter is stored locally in the app's private storage, protected by standard iOS data protection.
  • iCloud sync (optional). If you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings, an encrypted copy syncs through Apple's CloudKit to devices signed into the same Apple ID. Atlas never sees your Apple ID password.

Purchases & Bank Sync

This App Store release is free and does not offer subscriptions or in-app purchases. Bank connections are not offered in this release; accounts, bills, and activity can be added manually or by CSV import.

Profile Photo

If you add a profile photo, it is saved only on your device (and in your iCloud container if sync is enabled). It is never uploaded to Atlas.

Deleting Your Data

Delete all data anytime from Settings → Delete All Data, or by deleting the app. Turning off iCloud sync stops new sync; remove iCloud data from your Apple ID settings if needed.

Face ID & Touch ID

Optional app lock uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework entirely on your device. Atlas never receives or stores biometric data.

Notifications

Bill and cash-flow alerts are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

Analytics & Advertising

Atlas does not use advertising SDKs, IDFA, or cross-app tracking. If you explicitly enable Anonymous product analytics, Atlas uses PostHog to receive limited product events for activation and returning use. Events are grouped by an anonymous app-install identifier and may include the app version and a non-identifying campaign label.

Analytics events do not include names, email addresses, account identifiers, paycheck amounts, pay dates, bill names, due dates, balances, transactions, bank information, or other financial content. Analytics are optional and can be turned off in Settings.

Children's Privacy

Atlas is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will reflect the revision.
