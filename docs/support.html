Common Questions

  • How do I delete all my data? Settings → Delete All Data.
  • Does Atlas require a bank login? No. Manual entry is the default. Premium optionally supports Plaid through a relay you deploy.
  • How does sync work? Three options: iCloud (Settings), couples income merge (Tools), encrypted data pack (Premium).
  • What's Atlas Premium? Partial plans, what-if sliders, encrypted backup, simulators, and optional bank sync.
  • How do I restore Premium? Settings → Atlas Premium → Restore purchases.
  • Can I export my data? Yes — Settings → Export Data (CSV). Premium adds encrypted export.
