Common Questions

  • How do I delete all my data? Settings → Delete All Data.
  • Does Atlas require a bank login? No. Bank connections are not offered in this App Store release.
  • How does sync work? iCloud sync is available in Settings, and couples income merge is in Tools.
  • Does Atlas charge for features? No. This release is free and does not offer subscriptions or in-app purchases.
  • Can I export my data? Yes — use Settings → Export Data.
